# Neshama Demo Video Script (final, matches live as of main 2e9ac3d)

60 to 90 seconds. Screen recording of neshama.ca with voiceover. Record against seeded placeholder data, not the live feed.

### [0:00-0:10] Hook
**Show:** landing page, scroll slowly past hero
**Say:** "When someone in our community passes away, everyone wants to help, but no one knows where to start. That's why we built Neshama."

### [0:10-0:20] Listings
**Show:** click Listings, scroll the feed, type a name in search
**Say:** "Neshama brings obituaries from Toronto and Montreal's Jewish funeral homes into one place, updated throughout the day. Search by name."

### [0:20-0:35] How Can I Help?
**Show:** click How Can I Help?, show the three cards, click Bring or Order Food, scroll the vendor list
**Say:** "See someone you know? Tap How Can I Help. Bring or order food, send something thoughtful, or prepare for the shiva. Everything in one place, so helping is simple."

### [0:35-0:55] Organize Meals
**Show:** click Organize Shiva Meals, walk the four steps (1 Shiva Details, 2 Organizer Information, 3 Plan the Meal Schedule, 4 Review and Submit), then the success screen with share buttons
**Say:** "If you're the friend coordinating meals, it takes just a few minutes. Add the shiva details, your information, and the meal schedule, then share the link on WhatsApp. Friends sign up for a day. No accounts needed."

### [0:55-1:10] The Calendar
**Show:** open a shiva page, scroll to the meal calendar, show volunteer names
**Say:** "The meal calendar shows what's covered and what still needs help. Green means a day is covered, amber means it still needs a meal. The family sees it all at a glance. No duplicate casseroles."

### [1:10-1:20] Close
**Say (warm-channel master):** "Neshama. Every soul remembered, every life honoured. Built for our community, and always free for families."
**Say (social cut):** "Neshama. Every soul remembered, every life honoured. Try it at neshama.ca, and share it with someone who needs it."

## Quick cut (30 seconds, WhatsApp/Story)
**Show:** landing, feed, How Can I Help cards, meal calendar
**Say:** "Neshama brings together obituaries and shiva meal coordination for the Toronto and Montreal Jewish community. Find who you're looking for, organize meals in just a few minutes, and share the link. No accounts, free for families, just community. Neshama.ca."

## Recorder notes
- Seeded placeholder data only. No real family names on screen.
- The live nav also shows Shiva Essentials, About, and How It Works (which links to this demo page). Do not click How It Works during the take. Ignore the others.
- One imperfect take is right for this audience. If your recorded run lands near three minutes on the organize flow, you can say "three minutes" instead of "a few".
